THE COURSE

Skill builds on skill.

Each week combines a short artist lecture, a focused technique, a studio assignment, and a clear finish line.

01

Line + Value

Observation, contour, proportion, light, graphite, still life.

02

Space + Perspective

One-point, two-point, architecture, ink, cross-hatching.

03

Color + Watercolor

Mixing, harmonies, psychology, atmosphere, Impressionism.

04

Portrait + Portfolio

Facial structure, figure, gesture, master copy, exhibition.

Weekly rhythm

  1. Look — meet an artist and one important work.
  2. Learn — understand the week’s technique.
  3. Make — follow the studio steps.
  4. Reflect — check the rubric and document the result.
